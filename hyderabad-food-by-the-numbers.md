# Hyderabad food by the numbers

Hyderabad is famous for one dish, but the listing data shows a much broader city. IndiaRestros lists **61,528 places to eat in Hyderabad** (October 2026). Here is what those numbers say, and how to use them when choosing where to eat.

## Where the food is

The three neighbourhoods with the most listings are all in the western IT corridor:

| Area | Places |
|---|---|
| Kondapur | 3,429 |
| Madhapur | 2,927 |
| Serilingampally | 1,625 |

That is where the city's office workers eat, so it is also where cafes, delivery kitchens and quick-service chains open first. For older Hyderabad food, look beyond the totals at the Old City and areas like Mehdipatnam and Khairatabad.

## What kind of places

- 33,296 restaurants
- 7,467 cafes
- 5,251 quick bites
- 4,805 bakeries
- 3,234 food courts
- 3,102 sweet shops
- 2,098 delivery kitchens
- 1,165 bars and pubs

Only **1,083 are pure veg**, a much smaller share than in many western and southern cities.

## What the city eats

The most-listed cuisines are **North Indian (13,005)**, **Chinese (12,134)** and **Biryani (10,029)**, followed by South Indian (9,231), desserts, bakery, coffee and beverages. Middle Eastern (2,375) and Mughlai (2,998) are unusually strong, reflecting Hyderabad's Arabian-influenced dishes like mandi alongside the Nizami tradition.

## How complete is the data?

- 52,946 places list a phone number (86%)
- 44,607 have opening timings
- 15,766 have a menu on file
- 21,081 have photos

So for most places you can call ahead. For about a quarter you can read the menu before you go.

## Using the numbers to pick a place

1. **Filter by cuisine first**, then by area, since the corridor neighbourhoods are crowded with options.
2. **Prefer places with a menu on file** when you want to check prices or veg options.
3. **Call before you travel**, especially for small biryani houses whose hours change during Ramzan.
