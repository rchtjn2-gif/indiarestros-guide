# Indore food by the numbers

Indore has a reputation as India's street-food capital, and the listing data backs it up. IndiaRestros lists **13,136 places to eat in Indore** (October 2026). Here is what stands out.

## A street-food city

Street food is the **third most-listed cuisine** in Indore, with **1,629 places**, behind only North Indian (4,082) and Chinese (2,731). In most big cities, street food sits much further down the list. Sandwiches (1,576) come next, which anyone who has eaten at Indore's night markets will recognise.

## Chai and cafe culture

- **2,770 cafes** out of 13,136 places, a high share for a city of Indore's size
- **1,550 coffee** and **622 tea** listings
- Chai chains and local cafes recur through the listings, from tea-and-sandwich cafes to chai bars

## What kind of places

| Type | Count |

| Restaurants | 6,038 |
| Cafes | 2,770 |

| Bakeries | 933 |
| Delivery kitchens | 736 |
| Sweet shops | 477 |
| Dhabas | 424 |

**457 places are pure veg.** Many more serve vegetarian menus without being exclusively veg, so check the menu.

## Where the food is

Most listings, **9,973**, fall in Indore City itself, with smaller clusters in **Piplya Kumar (441)** and **Rau (438)**. Dhabas tend to sit on the highways out of the city.

## How good is the data?

- 11,440 places list a phone number (88%)
- 8,285 have timings
- 5,386 have a menu on file (41%, a higher share than many larger cities)
- 6,490 have photos

## Making the most of it

1. **For street food**, filter by cuisine and go in the evening; many places only open then.
2. **For cafes**, check timings, which vary widely.
3. **On a highway trip**, use the dhaba listings and see the [highway dhaba guide](highway-dhaba-guide.html).
