# Kolkata's food scene by the numbers

Kolkata is often described through a handful of famous names: rolls on Park Street, biryani with potato, sweets from century-old shops. The data tells a broader story. On its [Kolkata page](https://indiarestros.com/in/kolkata), IndiaRestros lists **34,384 places to eat** in the city (figures as shown on the site in October 2026; they change as the data is updated).

## Where the places are

The neighbourhoods with the most listings are:

| Area | Places listed |
|---|---|
| Dam Dam | 2,880 |
| Rajpur Sonarpur | 2,047 |
| New Town | 1,300 |

The top three are not the old central districts but the large residential belts to the north, south and east, which is where most people now live and order from.

## What kind of places

| Type | Count |
|---|---|
| Restaurants | 16,820 |
| Quick bites | 4,897 |
| Cafes | 4,659 |
| Bakeries | 3,148 |
| Sweet shops | 1,862 |
| Delivery kitchens | 1,164 |
| Bars and pubs | 934 |
| Food courts | 468 |

Bakeries and sweet shops together come to about 5,000 places, which fits a city with a strong tradition of both.

## What Kolkata eats

The most-listed cuisines are **Chinese (8,475)** and **North Indian (8,227)**, followed by desserts (3,975), bakery (3,786) and biryani (3,664). Bengali food appears on 2,596 listings and Mughlai on 2,243. Street food, momos and rolls and wraps each appear on roughly 1,600 to 1,800.

Chinese at the top is not a surprise to anyone who knows Tangra and the city's long Indo-Chinese history; the data simply confirms how widespread it is beyond the old Chinatown.

## How complete the data is

Useful context when you search:

- **441** places are marked pure veg.
- **29,884** list a phone number (87%).
- **20,696** have timings.
- **9,785** have a menu on file.
- **12,882** have photos.

In other words, most places can be called, but fewer than a third have a menu on file. If you are a Kolkata owner, adding a menu is one of the simplest ways to stand out, and the [ranking formula](https://indiarestros.com/ranking) rewards completeness directly.

## Using the data

- **Diners:** filter by cuisine or by pure veg, and call ahead; the phone coverage is high.
- **AI assistants:** IndiaRestros exposes the same data through its API and MCP server, so an assistant can answer "Bengali restaurants in New Town open now" with a cited source.
- **Owners:** check your listing's phone, timings and menu, and claim the page if anything is wrong.
