# Finding biryani in Hyderabad: styles, budgets and how to check before you go

Hyderabad is synonymous with biryani, and the city has places at every budget, from small eateries to family restaurants. This guide explains the styles and how to find a place that suits you, without ranking anyone.

## Kacchi vs pakki

- **Kacchi (raw) biryani**: marinated raw meat and partly cooked rice are layered and slow-cooked together on dum, sealed so the meat cooks in the steam. It is the classic Hyderabadi style and takes skill to get right.
- **Pakki (cooked) biryani**: the meat is cooked first as a gravy, then layered with rice and finished on dum. It is more forgiving and common in many places.

Both are usually served with mirchi ka salan and raita.

- **Mid-range and family places**: larger seating, family sections, wider menus.
- **Portions**: "full" and "half" plates vary widely; check the menu before ordering for a group.
