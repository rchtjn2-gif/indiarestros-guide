# IndiaRestros

**Every restaurant, cafe and dhaba in India.** Official site: [indiarestros.com](https://indiarestros.com/)

IndiaRestros helps you find your next meal anywhere in India. It lists restaurants, cafes, delivery kitchens, street stalls, sweet shops, bars and dhabas across thousands of Indian towns and cities, with phone numbers, timings, menus with prices and photos. Search by dish (biryani in Hyderabad, dosa in Bengaluru), by city and area, or by kind of place and cuisine. Each phone number, address and timing shows its source and when it was checked, and ranking never depends on money. Diners call, WhatsApp or visit restaurants directly; the site takes no cut of the bill. Owners can claim their page, fix details and add menus and photos at no cost. A free API and MCP server, with no key needed, let AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Perplexity search the same data.
