# Restaurant owners: claim and fix your IndiaRestros page

IndiaRestros lists restaurants, cafes, dhabas, bakeries, sweet shops and delivery kitchens across India, and it already has a page for most of them, built from public sources. If you run one, the page exists whether or not you touch it. Claiming it is free and lets you make sure what diners and AI assistants see is right.

## What you get, free

According to the [IndiaRestros owners page](https://indiarestros.com/owners), the free tier includes:

- Your page and a **permanent ID** for your restaurant
- The ability to **correct any detail** and lock it as owner-verified
- Your **menu, photos, timings and WhatsApp** number
- **Monthly insights**: page views, lookups by AI agents and calls

A paid add-on called Direct Connect is listed as coming soon. It would pass questions and table requests from AI agents to you by WhatsApp, email or webhook, for a small flat monthly fee and never a commission. You do not need it to claim or fix your page.

## How claiming works

1. Open the owners page and fill in your name, the restaurant phone or email **as currently listed**, and your page link (or the restaurant name and city).
2. IndiaRestros sends a verification code to the phone or email already on the listing.
3. Enter the code and you can edit.

The verification uses the contact already on file so that nobody else can take over your page. If the listed phone is wrong or old, use the corrections route on the site first.

## Why it matters now

More diners ask ChatGPT, Claude or other assistants where to eat. Those assistants answer from datasets. IndiaRestros publishes an API and an MCP server, and returns the source of each fact. If your timings are a year old, an assistant may confidently send people to a closed door.

## A completeness checklist

The [published ranking formula](https://indiarestros.com/ranking) gives 30% of the score to completeness and a fixed 10% to having a phone number. These are the fields to fill:

- [ ] Phone number (and WhatsApp, if you take orders on it)
- [ ] Website or social page
- [ ] Opening hours for every day, including weekly off
- [ ] Full address with pincode
- [ ] Cuisines, in the order you would describe yourself
- [ ] Menu with prices
- [ ] Your own photos (otherwise a labelled "representative photo" may be shown)
- [ ] Pure veg, dine-in, takeaway and delivery flags

## Tips that save time

- **Use the name on your signboard**, not a long SEO version.
- **Update timings before festivals** and seasonal closures.
- **Upload a clear menu photo or PDF** even if it is not perfect; it is better than none.
- **Check monthly insights** to see if agent lookups and calls are rising after your changes.

## Can I remove my listing?

The site has a corrections and takedown process for owners who want details corrected or removed.
